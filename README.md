## 📖 **Overview | نبذة عامة**

**COSMIC-324** is an advanced, open-source space mission simulation, orbital tracking, and sovereign compliance platform. It is designed to be a comprehensive alternative to commercial tools like STK and GMAT, integrating deep space dynamics with enterprise governance:

- 🌍 **3D Keplerian Orbital Dynamics** with **J2 Perturbation** (Earth oblateness effect) for high-precision orbit propagation.
- 🛰️ **Hybrid Predictive Routing** for dynamic satellite constellation management.
- 📡 **Real-time Data Fetching** from Celestrak (supports up to 5000 satellites).
- 📶 **6G Spectrum Analysis** (S-Band, Ku-Band, Ka-Band, 6G-THz) with jamming simulation.
- ⚖️ **Sovereign & Legal Compliance Unit** for cross-border corporate law and regulatory tracking.
- 🔑 **Enterprise Licensing & HSM Security** for scalable commercial and governmental deployments.
- 🛸 **Debris & Collision Risk Assessment** for space situational awareness.
